# L1 Self-Service: My Device Won't Set Up Properly

**Version:** 1.0  
**Date:** 07-08-2026  
**Status:** Draft  
**Audience:** End users (non-technical)

---

## What's Happening?

You've been issued a new device or your device is being refreshed, and the setup process has stopped or failed. You may see a message like "Device already enrolled" or an error code. **Don't worry — your data is safe, and this is fixable by the IT Service Desk.**

---

## What You Can Try

### Step 1: Restart Your Device
The simplest fix for most setup issues is a restart. 

- Click the **Start** button (bottom left) > click the **Power** icon > select **Restart**
- Allow the device to restart completely (2–5 minutes)
- Wait for it to finish; do not force shutdown or restart again during this time

### Step 2: Wait 15–20 Minutes
After restart, the setup process may resume automatically in the background. **Leave the device running and connected to the network.** Let it sit for 15–20 minutes without interrupting it. You may see a setup screen or progress bar — this is normal.

### Step 3: Sign Out and Sign Back In
If the setup screen disappears after waiting:

- Click **Start** > click your account icon (top right) > click **Sign out**
- Wait 30 seconds
- Sign back in with your username and password
- Wait another 2–3 minutes while it finishes loading

### Step 4: Check Your Settings (Optional)
To confirm your device is set up correctly:

- Click **Start** > type "Settings" and open the Settings app
- Click **Accounts** on the left
- Look for a section called **Work or school account** — you should see **one** account listed (usually showing your company email or name)
- If you see this, your setup is likely complete

---

## When to Contact the Service Desk

**Stop here and call IT right away if:**

- The restart doesn't help and the setup screen is still stuck after 30 minutes
- You see an error message with a code (write down the exact code and message)
- You cannot sign in even with the correct password
- You see messages like "Device already enrolled" or "Access denied"

---

## What to Tell the Service Desk

When you call, have these details ready:

- **Your device name** — Right-click the **Start** button > select **System** > look for "Device name" at the top
- **The exact error message or code you see** (if any) — screenshot it if possible
- **When you received the device** (today, yesterday, etc.)
- **What you've already tried** (e.g., "I restarted twice" or "I waited 20 minutes")

**Example:** "Hi, my device won't finish setup. The device name is LAPTOP-ABC123, and I see an error code that says 0x80180014. I've restarted twice and waited 20 minutes."

---

## Important Notes

- Your files and data are protected and safe during this process
- Do **not** factory reset or wipe your device yourself — the IT team will handle this if needed
- Do **not** try to remove your work account from Settings — let IT do this
- Setup can take 15–25 minutes; patience is key
- If you have multiple devices, tell IT which one is affected
