# Floor 6 Login and Slow Computer Help (L1 Self-Service)

Title; Version 1.0; Date 14 August 2026; Status Draft; Source Runbook 1.0

If your sign-in is slow, your computer feels slow after sign-in, or desktop shortcuts are missing, use these safe steps:

1. Keep your device on and connected to the network.
2. If you can sign in, continue working and note how long sign-in took.
3. If sign-in is very slow, wait for it to complete before trying again.
4. Do not make system changes or try repair steps unless IT asks you to.
5. Watch for IT updates. The current update plan is one update in 30 minutes, then every 60 minutes until stable.

These steps are to reduce disruption while IT applies and checks service changes.

Contact the Service Desk now if:
- You cannot sign in.
- Sign-in stays unusually slow.
- Your shortcuts are still missing.
- Work is blocked.

When you contact the Service Desk, provide:
- Your name
- Location: Floor 6
- Device name
- First time you saw the issue
- Issue type: sign-in failure, slow sign-in, slow computer, or missing shortcuts
- Any error message shown
