# Floor 6 Partner Update

## 1. What happened
On Monday morning, staff on Floor 6 Legal reported slow sign-in, slow computer performance after sign-in, and some missing desktop shortcuts. These issues began after a Friday software update that was sent to Floor 6 Legal devices only. This timing and scope are confirmed.

A separate review is also in progress about a Copilot response that appeared to reference an unfamiliar client matter. This is being handled as a different workstream from the performance disruption.

## 2. What we are doing now
We are applying targeted containment for the affected Floor 6 group and closely tracking whether sign-in and performance improve. We are checking impact on a small pilot set first, then watching new incident reports and looking for any unintended impact outside Floor 6.

The Copilot review is continuing in parallel under a separate governance process.

## 3. What is still open
The single final cause is still under review. Current evidence supports more than one possible explanation, so we are not closing root cause yet.

The Copilot review is also under review. No final conclusion has been issued.

## 4. What staff should do
Keep devices powered on and connected. If you can sign in, continue working and note unusual delays. If you cannot sign in, or work is blocked, contact the Service Desk and share your device name, first time seen, and any on-screen message.

## 5. Next update
Next update will be shared by lunch today. After that, updates will continue at regular intervals until the incident is stable.
