# All-Staff Notice — Credentials Vault Access Issue
**Date:** 2026-08-12
**From:** DWP IT Support — Finbridge Team

---

We are aware that some staff are unable to access the credentials vault following the recent Win11 migration. We know this is affecting your work and we are sorry for the disruption. Our team is actively investigating and working to restore access as quickly as possible.

**While we work on a fix, please try the following:**
1. Sign out completely, wait 60 seconds, then sign back in.
2. Try a different browser or an incognito window.

These steps have helped some users get back in. They may not work for everyone, but they are worth trying before raising a ticket.

**We will share an update by end of day today.** We will not make promises on a fix time until we have more information, but we will keep you informed as things progress.

In the meantime, please do not raise duplicate tickets if you have already reported this — we have your report and it is being looked at.

Thank you for your patience.

— IT Support Team
