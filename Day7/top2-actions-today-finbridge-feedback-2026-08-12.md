# Top 2 Themes to Act on Today — Finbridge Staff Feedback
**Date:** 2026-08-12

---

## #1 — Credentials Vault Inaccessible

Three people are locked out and it has been three days. Your whole team cannot work without this.

**Try first:**
1. Sign out completely, wait 60 seconds, then sign back in.
2. Try a different browser or an incognito window.

**Contact the service desk if it still does not open.** Tell them: your name, that the credentials vault shows an error since the Win11 migration, how many people are affected, and the exact error message you see.

---

## #2 — Admin Console Lockouts

Multiple engineers cannot get into the admin console, which means support work across the team is stalling.

**Try first:**
1. Clear your browser cache and cookies, then try again.
2. Check you are connected to the VPN before opening the console.

**Contact the service desk if you are still locked out.** Tell them: your username, when it started, and whether anyone else on your team has the same problem.
