# Leadership Update: Windows 11 Migration — End-User Impact & Status
**Report Date:** 2026-08-12

---

## Overall Migration Picture

Our Windows 11 rollout has reached the recovery phase. During the upgrade week (Aug 1–6), we identified and resolved issues across email, remote access, cloud file synchronization, printers, and device performance for approximately 215 Finance users and smaller groups across the organization. The majority of these issues were diagnosed and fixed within 48–72 hours of report; no data loss occurred and no critical operations were permanently disrupted. End users experienced temporary inconvenience—email delays, slow system performance, and VPN connection failures on individual devices—but business continuity was maintained through focused incident response and targeted remediation. Most users are now operating normally on Windows 11 with no ongoing service degradation from the upgrade itself.

## Open Items & Next Actions

One area still requiring attention is device startup speed, which has slowed by approximately 25 seconds for Finance users following a mandatory security policy applied on August 4. This slowdown does not block access but affects daily user experience at login. We have identified three likely technical causes and are currently running targeted diagnostics to confirm which specific policy or configuration is responsible. A fix is expected within 5 business days—either through policy tuning or configuration refinement. All other reported issues from the migration wave have been closed and validated as resolved. We continue to monitor endpoint health across all upgraded devices for any emerging patterns.

---

**Key Takeaway:** Migration is substantially complete. User issues are isolated, under active investigation, and being addressed proactively.
