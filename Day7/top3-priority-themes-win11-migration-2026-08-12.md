# Top 3 Themes to Act on Today (Win11 Post-Migration)

Method used: Prioritization weighs both impact severity and comment volume. Blockers are weighted above Friction/Minor, so lower-volume Blockers can outrank higher-volume Friction themes.

## 1) Authentication lockouts and AVD sign-in failures (Count: 7, Severity: Blocker)
Why it ranks here:
- Highest-impact category (users cannot sign in/work) and also high volume.
- Includes both AVD access failure and repeated account lockouts, indicating a broad authentication stability issue.

One sentence for manager:
- "Authentication and AVD sign-in failures are the top operational risk today because they fully block work for multiple users and are occurring repeatedly."

## 2) Missing files and OneDrive sync concerns (Count: 5, Severity: Blocker)
Why it ranks here:
- Direct risk of data unavailability during business-critical tasks (reports/meetings), which is work-stopping.
- Volume is substantial for a Blocker and carries high business and confidence impact.

One sentence for manager:
- "Missing files and OneDrive sync failures are our second priority because they create immediate business continuity risk when users cannot retrieve required documents."

## 3) Shared drive access denied (S:/Finance) (Count: 3, Severity: Blocker)
Why it ranks here:
- Lower volume than some Friction themes, but Blocker impact is severe since users cannot access core team shares.
- Explicit reporting of blocked month-end/reporting tasks elevates urgency despite fewer comments.

One sentence for manager:
- "Shared drive access denials are third priority because although fewer users reported it, affected staff are fully blocked from critical Finance workflows."

---

Not selected in top 3 today:
- Floor 3 printer mapping failure (Count: 6, Friction): high volume but generally workaround available (print on floor 2), so below Blockers.
- VPN instability (Count: 4, Friction) and slow logon (Count: 3, Friction): significant productivity drag but less severe than work-stopping access/data issues.
