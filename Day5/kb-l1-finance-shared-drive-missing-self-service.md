# My S: Drive Is Missing — What To Do

**For:** Finance team members  
**Type:** Self-service guide

---

## Don't worry — your files are safe

If your S: drive has disappeared, your files are still on the system. Nothing has been deleted. This is a known issue that the IT team are aware of and can fix quickly. Follow the steps below first, and if the drive is still missing, contact the Service Desk.

---

## Step 1 — Sign out and sign back in

This is the most effective first step and fixes the issue for most people.

1. Save and close any work you have open
2. Click the **Start** button (bottom left of your screen)
3. Click your **name or profile picture**
4. Select **Sign out**
5. Wait 30 seconds, then sign back in as normal
6. Check whether the **S: drive** has reappeared in File Explorer

> If the S: drive is back — great, you're all set. No need to contact anyone.

---

## Step 2 — Wait a few minutes and try again

If signing out and back in did not bring the drive back straight away, wait **5 minutes** and then repeat Step 1. Sometimes the system needs a short time to finish setting up after you sign in.

---

## Step 3 — Restart your computer

If the drive is still missing after Steps 1 and 2:

1. Save and close all open work
2. Click **Start → Power → Restart** (not Shut down)
3. Wait for your computer to fully restart and sign back in
4. Check whether the S: drive has reappeared

---

## Still missing? Contact the Service Desk

If you have tried all three steps and the S: drive is still not showing, please contact the IT Service Desk. You do not need to do anything else yourself.

**When you call or raise a ticket, please mention:**

- Your name and your device name (shown at the bottom of the login screen, usually starts with `DESKTOP-`)
- The date and time you first noticed the drive was missing
- Which steps above you have already tried
- Whether anyone else in your team has the same issue
