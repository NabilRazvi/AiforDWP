# Help Guide: Black Screen After Sign-In

Version: v 1.0  
Date: 07/08/2026  
Status: Draft

## What this means
If you sign in and only see a black screen, do not worry. Your files and work are safe. This is usually a short sign-in problem, not a lost-file problem.

## What you can do now
1. Wait 30 seconds on the black screen.
You should see: Your normal work screen appears.

2. If nothing changes, sign out of the work app and close it.
You should see: The app is fully closed.

3. Open the work app again and sign in again.
You should see: Your work screen opens normally.

4. If the black screen appears again, sign out once more.
You should see: You are signed out.

5. Wait 2 minutes before trying again.
You should see: The next sign-in may work.

6. Try one final sign-in.
You should see: You can get into your work screen.

7. If it still fails, stop retrying and contact the Service Desk.
You should see: Your issue is sent to support quickly.

## Important reassurance
- Your documents and mailbox are still stored safely.
- Signing in again does not delete your files.
- This issue does not mean your work account is broken.

## Contact the Service Desk if...
Contact the Service Desk if the screen stays black after 3 sign-in tries, or if you keep getting signed out.

## Details to include in your support request
Please include:
- Your full name
- Your work email address (or username)
- Best phone number to reach you
- Your location (office/home)
- Time the problem started
- How many times you tried to sign in
- Whether it worked after waiting 30 seconds
- A screenshot (picture) of the black screen, if possible
- Any error message shown (exact wording)
